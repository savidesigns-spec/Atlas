The rules, in plain English

No fine print. Here's all of it.

1

Kickbacks are credits, not cash

They're bonus platform credits you can spend on any model. They can't be withdrawn, sold or transferred.

2

This is not an investment

A founder seat is not equity or ownership, and kickbacks aren't tied to company revenue or profit. They depend only on your own usage.

3

Kickback credits last 60 days

They're spent after your plan credits and expire 60 days after they arrive. Your regular credits keep the normal rollover rules.

4

Cancel and the lock ends

You can cancel any time in one click. If you do, you give up your founder price and seat, and they don't come back.

5

Switch plans freely

Move up or down between plans and keep founder pricing on each. Your tenure on the ladder carries over.

6

10,000 means 10,000

The counter is live. When it hits zero, founder pricing closes for good.

FAQ

Questions

Why pay more than the regular price?

Three reasons: your price never goes up while regular prices can, you get a growing share of your spend back, and you get founder-only perks. To be straight about the math: at full use and the top 20% rate, kickbacks are worth roughly three-quarters of the founder premium in credit value. The price lock and perks are the rest of the deal.

When do I get my first kickback?

On the 1st of the month after your first full billing month. It's based on the credits you spent in that month.

Do kickback credits count toward the next kickback?

No. Kickbacks are calculated on plan, bonus pack and top-up credits you spend, not on kickback credits.

What if I pause or my card fails?

You have a 14-day grace period to fix a failed payment and keep your seat. After that the seat is released.

Is this available outside the US?

Yes. Prices are in USD and local tax may apply.
