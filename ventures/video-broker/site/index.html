FAQ

Questions

Why is the first plan $75 and not $9?

We're built for people who ship content for a business: brands, freelancers and agencies. $75 gets you 6,000 credits, store sync, 25 automations and agent access. If you only want to play with a few images a month, a cheaper consumer app will suit you better, and that's fine.

What can 6,000 credits make?

About 66 Kling 3.0 clips, or ~270 Nano Banana Pro images, or 250 Veo 3.1 Lite clips. Twice that on the Saver queue. Mix and match freely.

What is the Saver queue?

Half-price generation for anything that doesn't need to be instant. Jobs finish within 24 hours, usually much sooner. Automations use it by default.

Is Founding Member an investment?

No. It's a loyalty program. You pay a founder price and get bonus platform credits back each month based on your own usage. There's no cash, no ownership and no share of company revenue.

Can my AI agent spend my money?

Only within the caps you set for that agent's key. Agents can't buy top-ups. They can only send you a payment link.

Who owns what I make?

You do, subject to each model provider's terms, which we list on every model page.
